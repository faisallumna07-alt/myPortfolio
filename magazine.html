Personal Growth Magazine

An Editorial Look Into College Life, Technology, and Self-Discipline

Overview

This self-authored digital editorial magazine serves as a personal reflection on my journey as a Computer Science student. It captures a candid snapshot of college life, balancing technical exploration in software development with the physical discipline found in fitness and gym training. Beyond structured routines and academic expectations, the publication explores what it means to find one's own path, embrace consistency, and shape a future that genuinely aligns with personal values.

What I Learned

Crafting this publication gave me the opportunity to expand my skills in visual communication, digital publishing, and editorial layout design. I learned how to structure compelling visual stories through intentional typography, cohesive color palettes, and balanced spacing.

On a personal level, putting this project together reminded me of the importance of self-reflection. It reinforced the idea that personal growth does not follow a strict script—it is built through daily habits, patience, and staying open to learning both inside and outside the traditional classroom.
