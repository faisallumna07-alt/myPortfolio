Student Attrition & Stress Analysis

Data Mining & Visual Exploration of Student Performance Using Orange Data Mining

Overview

This project utilizes Orange Data Mining to analyze a comprehensive student dataset (`LAB-ACT-1_DATASET.csv`) containing records on student demographics, study habits, attendance rates, GPAs, stress levels, and dropout status[cite: 44, 45]. The primary goal of this data workflow is to uncover the root causes of student attrition and evaluate how academic pressure, study duration, and attendance impact academic success[cite: 45].

Using visual programming nodes such as File, Edit Domain, Impute, Select Columns, Distributions, and Scatter Plots, the workflow maps relationships between variables to isolate the strongest predictors of student dropouts[cite: 44, 45].

What Was Analyzed

  • Data Preparation & Domain Editing: Cleaned missing values using the Impute widget and transformed numerical target labels (0/1) into clear categorical values ("No" / "Yes") using Edit Domain to avoid confusion[cite: 45].
  • Dropout Distribution: Examined student dropout rates distributed across departments and scholarship tiers[cite: 45].
  • Attendance vs. GPA Correlation: Evaluated whether high attendance rates directly guarantee high academic standing[cite: 45].
  • Study Hours vs. GPA Correlation: Analyzed the effectiveness of long study hours in relation to student grade point averages[cite: 45].
  • Stress Index vs. GPA: Explored how academic strain and low GPA contribute to heightened student stress and potential attrition[cite: 45].

Findings & Key Insights

  • GPA is the Primary Attrition Driver: The analysis reveals that academic performance (GPA) is the strongest indicator of student dropout. Many students struggle with low GPA despite maintaining high attendance rates[cite: 45].
  • Study Hours Don't Guarantee Success: Scatter plot evaluations showed that long study hours do not automatically translate to high GPAs. Students who spend excessive hours studying but fail to comprehend material experience heightened frustration and dropout risks[cite: 45].
  • The Stress-GPA Loop: Lower GPAs strongly correlate with an elevated stress index. The combination of academic struggle and high stress forms the primary catalyst for student attrition[cite: 45].

What I Learned

Building this Orange workflow deepened my understanding of exploratory data analysis and visual data mining. I learned how crucial data preprocessing steps—like handling missing values and mapping categorical domains—are before generating reliable visualizations[cite: 45].

More importantly, this project highlighted that student retention efforts should focus less on superficial engagement metrics (such as mere attendance or study duration) and more on targeted academic support and mental health interventions to help students efficiently comprehend complex material[cite: 45].
