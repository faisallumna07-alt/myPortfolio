Felivet Insight

AI-Powered Feline Skin Disease Detection

Overview

FeliVet Insight is an AI-powered mobile application that uses YOLO11n to detect ringworm, scabies, and flea allergy dermatitis in cats. It provides detection results, confidence scores, and preliminary triage guidance to help cat owners assess their pets' skin conditions.

Main Features of the Application

The application incorporates several functions to support preliminary feline skin disease assessment:

  • Image Capture and Upload – Allows users to take photographs of their cat's affected skin using the smartphone camera or select existing images from the gallery.
  • AI-Based Disease Detection – Uses YOLO11n to detect and classify three selected feline skin diseases:
    • Ringworm
    • Scabies
    • Flea Allergy Dermatitis
  • Confidence Score – Displays the model's confidence value for each detected skin condition.
  • Preliminary Triage Classification – Categorizes the apparent urgency of the condition into three levels:
    • Stable – Indicates a condition with a lower apparent level of urgency.
    • Moderate – Indicates a condition that may require veterinary attention.
    • High – Indicates a condition with a higher apparent level of urgency.
  • Non-Diagnostic Guidance – Provides preliminary information to help cat owners understand the appropriate level of care to consider.
